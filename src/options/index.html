Coverage and privacy

Standard is faster. Deep DOM is useful for deeply nested Elementor pages.

The intent only describes why the capture was made. It does not run UX rules.

Strict removes the most identity metadata. Diagnostic retains more bounded technical identifiers.

Includes elements hidden by display, visibility, or opacity while remaining bounded by the limits.

Query strings, fragments, and credentials are never included.

Page titles may reveal private content. Leave disabled unless needed for matching.

Exports counts, wrapping, clipping, and line-box facts without raw text.

Exports tag, role, disabled state, tabindex, pointer-events, and similar raw facts.

Exports real DOM parent, nearest emitted parent, positioned, scroll, and clipping ancestors.

Form values and contenteditable text are excluded. Remaining previews are redacted and length-limited.
