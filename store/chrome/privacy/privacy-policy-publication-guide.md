# Privacy-policy publication guide

1. Review the repository `PRIVACY.md` and the privacy documents in this directory against the exact production build.
2. Add the legal entity name, jurisdiction-specific contact details, effective date, and revision process with qualified legal review.
3. Publish the policy at a stable HTTPS URL accessible without authentication.
4. Replace `PRIVACY_POLICY_PUBLIC_URL_REQUIRED` in store administration records only. Do not add administrative placeholders to production extension files.
5. Enter the same public URL in the Chrome Web Store dashboard.
6. Verify the page is reachable in a private browser session and remains available throughout review and distribution.
7. Recheck the policy after any permission or evidence-category change.
